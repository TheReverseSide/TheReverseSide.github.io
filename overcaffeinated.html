Overcaffeinated

Personal Project | 2024

Check it out on Github

Python Numpy Matplotlib PyQt5

This just started out as a silly idea that ended up coming to reality. I have always been curious how much I am sabotaging my sleep in order to stay alert during the day, especially during Finnish winters. The bedtime is also customizable, so you can check how much caffeine you'll (approximately) have in your system when you go to sleep.

I included some metabolic variation, because plenty of people I know claim to metabolize it quickly (Let's remember the Better-than-average effect, though).


DISCLAIMER: Caffeine is notoriously difficult to measure, as are individual metabolisms, making this an approximation just for fun, nothing too precise. I used a half life of 5 ± 1.5 hours, assuming a healthy normal population.
