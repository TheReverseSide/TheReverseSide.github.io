Afterdark VR Movie

Helsinki, Finland, 2022

After Dark Helsinki Oy


A freelancing project created to premiere a 180 degree VR video for a film festival.


This was a solo project, I used Unity, C#, the AVpro video package and Oculus Dev Kit to create the project for Oculus Quests 1 & 2.

Battle Beetles

Helsinki, Finland, 2021


A PC battle royale where players ride beetles in an arena, using a second-person camera that follows the beetle, not the character. Don't get knocked off!


I wrote code for the UI, various gameplay elements, and controller input.


Student project for the Game Project course at Aalto University, developed in 3 months using Unity. Team Size: 6
