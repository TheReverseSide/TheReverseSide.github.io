Marmortal

A FPS with fluid movement, wall-running and frantic race up a tower against the clock.

Runner up of the 2021 Bit1 game competition!


Programming role:

I was responsible for programming the logic for sound effects and game music, using the FMOD sound engine with Unity and C#. I also handled programming and setting up the UI in Unity.


User research role:

I designed and conducted moderated playtests, wrote and distributed surveys, and presented findings with the development team. As we moved closer to release, my research shifted focus onto usability issues and control nuances, ensuring the game "felt" as intended. This research directly impacted the development of the movement system, particularly the wallrunning mechanic.


Technology / Team / Time:

Developed in 7 months using Unity for PC. Team Size: 10.
