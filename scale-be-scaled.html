Scale or Be Scaled

Please the Doggod! Race against your opponent to get your scale balanced better than your opponent's before time runs out. You can interfere with your opponent in plenty of ways, just be sure to protect your own scale as well!

Developed over a long weekend using Unity for PC and WebGL. Team Size: 4 (2 artists, 2 programmers)

The Doggod requires things to be balanced!! Do not disappoint them.


My roles:

Unity C# programming language Gameplay programming

My programming focuses were on gameplay (particularly interacting with items, activating powerups, etc), scoring logic, scale physics, and UI.


Project management Notion

I also acted as the project manager, keeping the task list organized and prioritized, and ensuring everyone was on target.


Players attempt to balance their scales, while collecting powerups and avoiding the other player's interference.

Powerful powerups such as bombs can quickly change the tide of the game!
